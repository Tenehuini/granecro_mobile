      Quit the game?

      You can continue it later from the start screen.

      How to play

      Goal

      You are a necromancy student. When the cards of both semesters run out, you graduate if:

      1. Your thesis is finished (5 of 5).
      2. You earned the credits required by the chosen difficulty.
      3. Your sanity is not negative (0 or more).

      Setup

      The cards are shuffled: 12 form Semester 1 (14 with the expansion) and the rest form the Semester 2 deck. You start with 3 sanity.

      Each turn

      A card is turned and you choose what to do with it:

      • Enroll yourself to the course: pay the sanity shown at the top (and thesis, for W courses), earn the credits shown at the bottom and learn the magic the course teaches. Sanity can go down to −2.
      • Leave for the next semester: only during Semester 1. The card goes to the Semester 2 deck, which holds up to 12 cards (14 with the expansion).
      • Raise Study Buddy: place the card under a course of the current semester that has a free slot and get its effect: the 1st study buddy gives what the single card shows; the 2nd, what the two cards show.
      • Work on thesis: +1 thesis.
      • Recover sanity point: only with Sanity boost cards (the spiral). +1, up to 5.
      • Just discard the card.

      Course prerequisites

      • Magic: some courses require ▼ dark or ○ light magic. Each learned magic covers only one requirement. Courses showing ▼○ at the bottom teach a magic that counts as either one.
      • A1 / A2: Soul mending requires A1 (Speaking with the dead) and Invoking shadowspawns requires A2 (History of the Void).

      Tired

      Tired cards can't be used to work on your thesis or as study buddies: just discard them. In the expansion, W courses accept only Tired cards as study buddies.

      Between semesters

      When Semester 1 ends you recover 3 sanity (up to 5) and the Semester 2 deck is shuffled. Study buddies can only be placed under courses of the current semester.

      Card symbols

      🌀 spiral: sanity · ✒️ quill: thesis · C: credits · ▼ dark magic · ○ light magic.

      Difficulty

      Credits needed to graduate: Easy 35, Medium 40, Hard 45. With the expansion: 45, 50 and 52.

      Cards

      Press and hold any card to see it bigger. Tap it to see all its details. Full rules at bulgur007.itch.io.
